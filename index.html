JibonSetu — Blood Donation Platform

When someone needs blood, the hardest part is finding a willing donor quickly. JibonSetu lets people search donors by blood group, district and upazila, call or message donors who allow it, and post blood requests that donors can accept. Patient details stay private, and a donation counts only when the requester confirms it. The app works in Bangla and English, in light and dark mode.

App tour

What is inside

Privacy by designDonors choose to be listed and decide who can contact them: everyone, or only people whose request they accepted.
Emergency priorityRequests are marked Normal, Urgent or Emergency, and urgent ones appear first on the home screen.
NotificationsAn in-app inbox shows new responses and request progress.
Admin dashboardAdmins manage users, donors and requests, review reports and flags, verify donations, and see an action history.
Bangla and EnglishOne tap switches the whole interface. Light and dark modes follow the device setting.
Safety remindersThe app warns users never to pay for blood and to meet in public places like hospitals.
